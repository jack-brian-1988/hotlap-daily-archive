HOW THE RANKING WORKS

The rating is μ − 3σ: estimated skill minus an uncertainty margin. Higher is stronger; lower σ means more confidence. Only active drivers with at least ten recorded races receive an active rank. Provisional and inactive drivers keep their ratings and remain searchable in their own views.

A driver becomes inactive after 30 complete UTC days without a valid classified lap. The rating is retained, with no inactivity point deduction. A new recorded race restores activity when the source data is updated. Names identify drivers; the API does not expose authenticated account activity.

Ratings are read from the externally stored results when this page opens. All classified participants were used, including positions outside the top ten. The Standings date filter does not affect TrueSkill.
