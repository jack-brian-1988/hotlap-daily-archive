HOW TRUESKILL WORKS

A skill estimate based on who you beat and who beats you. New drivers can climb without matching years of accumulated points.

The rating

TrueSkill tracks estimated skill (μ) and uncertainty (σ). The displayed rating is μ − 3σ: a conservative score that rewards strong results and confidence in the estimate. Higher is stronger; lower σ means more confidence.

How races are calculated

Every completed UTC race is processed in chronological order, using the full classification in one multiplayer TrueSkill update. Finishing position and the strength of all opponents matter, rather than points or lap-time gaps. All classified participants count, including positions outside the top ten.

The stored API finishing order is preserved, even when lap times match. Draw probability is zero. Missing a race is not treated as a loss.

Activity and eligibility

  • Active ranking: at least 10 recorded race days and fewer than 30 complete UTC days since the last valid result.
  • Provisional: fewer than 10 recorded race days. Ratings remain visible in the provisional view.
  • Inactive: 30 or more complete UTC days without a valid recorded result. The driver leaves the active ranking but keeps their rating.

Inactivity does not deduct rating or add extra uncertainty over calendar time. A new recorded result restores activity when the source data is updated; the 10-race requirement still applies.

Data and scope

Results come from the game API and are stored externally to avoid unnecessary API calls. This page reads the saved ratings when opened. The Standings date filter does not affect TrueSkill.

Driver names identify results, rather than authenticated accounts. Distinct aliases stay separate, except for the known duplicate Hoozah! alias. Active ranks use full-precision ratings; values are displayed to two decimal places.
